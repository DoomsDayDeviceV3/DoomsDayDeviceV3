About Me

I'm an engineer and researcher designing, building, and analyzing intelligent robotic systems. I specialize in multidisciplinary development, integrating advanced control, embedded systems, mechatronics, and human-robot interaction.

I care about how systems actually behave in real environments, beyond descriptions on paper. I develop robust, physical machines and look for elegant solutions.

My work spans both theory and application across a wide range of disciplines, from underactuated humanoid control to wearables for healthcare.

Currently, I am researching robot foundation models, as well as the control and design of world-leading nanorobots.

Beyond technical design, I have experience leading robotics initiatives, guiding engineering teams, and organizing large-scale projects.
